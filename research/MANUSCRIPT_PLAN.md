# Manuscript plan

Working title: **A Reproducible Framework for Reliability and Data Quality in Cloud-Native Data Pipelines**

Status: research in progress; not peer reviewed.

## Proposed structure
1. Abstract
2. Introduction and problem statement
3. Related work
4. Reliability framework
5. Experimental design
6. Results
7. Threats to validity
8. Practical implications
9. Conclusion
10. References
11. Artifact availability

## Claims policy
The manuscript may claim only what is supported by generated experiment outputs and cited literature. It must not claim employer/client adoption or impact without independently documented permission and evidence.

## Authorship
Authorship should reflect genuine intellectual and writing contributions. AI-assisted drafting, coding, or editing must be disclosed if required by the target venue and must comply with that venue's current policy.

## Submission
Select a legitimate peer-reviewed venue only after the experiments and manuscript are mature. Check the venue's current scope, formatting, preprint, artifact, and AI-use policies immediately before submission.
