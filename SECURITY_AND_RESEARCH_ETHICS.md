# Security, confidentiality, and research integrity

This repository is an independent open-source project.

Do not contribute:
- proprietary employer or client code
- confidential datasets or schemas
- internal screenshots, tickets, architecture, credentials, identifiers, or metrics
- copied implementations that the contributor is not authorized to publish

Research results must be reproducible from the public repository or clearly documented public dependencies. Negative and failed results should not be concealed. Citations must be checked against original sources. No citation, experiment, benchmark, review, acceptance, or publication status may be fabricated.

Generative AI may assist with development and editing, but human authors remain responsible for technical correctness, originality, citations, disclosure requirements, and venue policy compliance.
