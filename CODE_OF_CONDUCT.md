# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, professional, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing levels of experience.
- Do not share confidential or proprietary information.
- Give credit for ideas and contributions.
- Keep technical discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, or deliberate disclosure of confidential information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior materially violates these expectations.
